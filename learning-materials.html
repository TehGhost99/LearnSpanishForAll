Materiales de aprendizaje

EN: Work top to bottom. Lessons 1–12 build your first ~500-word foundation. Lessons 13–15 go deeper.

ES: Trabaja de arriba abajo. Las lecciones 1–12 construyen ~500 palabras. Las 13–15 profundizan.

Part 1 — Core foundation / Base

  1. Greetings & Sounds — Sounds, hola, courtesy, and first conversations.
  2. Who I Am — Name, origin, and simple self-introductions.
  3. Numbers, Time & Calendar — Numbers, clock time, days, and months.
  4. Family & People — Family words and describing people.
  5. Home & Class — Objects around home and classroom.
  6. Food & Drink — Meals, ordering, and food vocabulary.
  7. Daily Routines (Present -ar) — Daily habits with common -ar verbs.
  8. Places & ir — Places in town and using ir.
  9. Weather & Clothes — Weather phrases, seasons, and clothing.
  10. Likes & Hobbies (gustar) — Me gusta and free-time talk.
  11. Shopping & Needs — Querer, necesitar, comprar, and prices.
  12. Core 500 Checkpoint — Review and solidify your first ~500 words.

Part 2 — Going deeper / Más profundidad

  1. Past Basics (pretérito) — Tell simple past stories with high-frequency verbs.
  2. Travel & Going Out — Restaurant, directions, and travel phrases.
  3. Plans & Going Deeper — Ir a + infinitive and next-step fluency habits.
