## Methods & standards / Métodos y estándares

- [ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines (2012)](https://www.actfl.org/uploads/files/general/ACTFLProficiencyGuidelines2012.pdf) — Novice → Intermediate can-do framing
- [NCSSFL-ACTFL Can-Do Statements](https://www.actfl.org/educator-resources/ncssfl-actfl-can-do-statements) — functional goals
- [Krashen — Comprehension Hypothesis Extended](https://sdkrashen.com/content/articles/comprehension_hypothesis_extended.pdf) — comprehensible input
- [Duolingo Method white paper](http://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/duolingo-method-whitepaper.pdf) — spaced repetition in app learning
- [Nakata & Elgort (2021) — spacing in vocabulary learning](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0267658320927764)
- [Retrieval practice vs imitation (Karpicke et al. lineage / Kang & others)](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-013-0450-z)
- [Vista Higher Learning — CI / ADI classroom white paper](https://go.vistahigherlearning.com/rs/156-GRO-136/images/encuentros_2026_ci-adi-whitepaper.pdf?version=0)
- [National Spanish Exam / NSA Level 01 vocabulary themes](https://nationalspanishexam.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/NSE-NSA-Vocabulary-2023-24-Level-01.pdf) — beginner topic coverage
